My recommended synchronization strategy
Start with direct peer-to-peer synchronization over your home or office Wi-Fi. Your Windows PC and MacBook can exchange encrypted changes without Google Cloud.

For devices on different networks, introduce secure peer-to-peer connectivity or a small relay service only when necessary. An optional cloud backup can provide disaster recovery independently of synchronization.

This approach keeps cloud costs close to zero for the core application, although external APIs, remote AI models, and optional storage may still incur charges.

Most importantly, the PRD now explicitly instructs the coding LLM not to provision Google Cloud or build a hosted backend for V1. The application must work locally first, while keeping its data model ready for future synchronization.
