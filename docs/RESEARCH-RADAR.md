# Research radar

Discover now combines the last approved feed snapshots with up to 100 retained research-query snapshots. The radar deduplicates exact URLs using the newest refresh, with a maximum of 3,000 cached discoveries. It never fetches a source until the user approves the normal refresh, installs a model or simulator, or operates hardware.

Robotics presets cover aquatic ROVs, aerial drones, ground navigation, humanoids/manipulation and soft/embodied robotics. Agriculture presets cover irrigation/soil sensing, precision farming/crop intelligence, farm robots, solar pumps and scheme research. Publisher directories/bookmarks were checked against [MIT robotics](https://news.mit.edu/topic/robotics), [NASA RSS sources](https://www.nasa.gov/rss-feeds/), [USDA ARS RSS](https://www.ars.usda.gov/news-events/rss-feeds/), [USDA NASS syndication](https://data.nass.usda.gov/Newsroom/Syndication/News/index.php), [India myScheme](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/) and the [PM-KUSUM portal](https://pmkusum.mnre.gov.in/). Directory links are not represented as feeds; only explicitly listed feed URLs prepare an RSS refresh. Source availability can change. Scheme bookmarks do not assert eligibility or an open application window.

Topic attention uses matching title/description terms. Its displayed score is recent publications (last 14 days) × distinct provider types in the matching cache, plus positive measured-count observations. Days 14–28 form the comparison window; missing/future dates are excluded. This is a local sample heuristic, not global market growth or verified research quality.

Measured-growth ranks recompute changes per day from snapshot deltas and elapsed hours. Percentiles compare only equal provider/metric cohorts; ties receive midpoint ranks, lone observations have no percentile, and observations older than 14 days are excluded. Stars, subscribers and download counts are never added together. Provider rounding and rolling windows remain visible limitations.

Creator opportunities use shared title terms across at least two videos and two reported channel names. Names are not verified identities. Suggestions can be saved as editable Content Studio drafts with source URLs and an evidence-checking outline. Research topics similarly create editable, evidence-linked Idea Lab records. These actions save locally; they do not publish, execute tools or send anything to AI.

Thirteen synthetic analytics assertions exercise deduplication, publication windows, token boundaries, metric isolation, percentile/rate calculation, stale observations and cross-creator evidence. Real feeds still need internet/source availability; YouTube needs a key. Native QA coverage is recorded separately.

Selected isolated Mac QA on 2026-11-16 verified synthetic topic counts/score, local research and creator draft saving, and arXiv query preparation with fetch consent still off. No live provider search was made.
