# Public business discovery

Open **Workspaces → Opportunities & CRM → Find public business listings**. Select a public search point, category and radius, approve the public read, then search. No command-center account, provider sign-in, API key or local model is required. The app does not read device location.

The automatic plan uses Dresel’s North America replica first for North American coordinates, then Photon. Elsewhere it uses Photon, then VK Maps Overpass. These destinations are disclosed before approval. A custom Overpass endpoint uses only that endpoint. Regional Overpass services are listed in the [OpenStreetMap provider directory](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Overpass_API#Public_Overpass_API_instances); Photon’s operator permits reasonable public use in its [project documentation](https://github.com/komoot/photon#demo-server). The app caches successful searches and limits manual requests. Provider availability and map freshness remain external dependencies.

The old default endpoint failed or refused requests from this machine. Regional Overpass sends a short bounding-box query; global Photon sends a category/radius lookup through its [documented public reverse endpoint](https://github.com/komoot/photon/blob/master/docs/api-v1.md). Both paths validate OSM identity, category and coordinates, then filter candidate points or way centres to the chosen circular radius locally. It returns at most 50 matching named listings, with original OSM links, websites when present, approximate distances, fetch time and attribution. Unknown coordinates and partial/warning responses are excluded. The Overpass query approach follows the [Overpass bounding-box documentation](https://dev.overpass-api.de/overpass-doc/en/full_data/bbox.html).

A transient transport/5xx failure gets one retry after two seconds; then the next declared provider may be used. At most two providers are considered, and the whole lookup is capped at 75 seconds. Access refusals and rate limits stop the lookup; Retry-After pauses are respected. **Stop lookup** cancels outstanding work. Custom endpoints still require public HTTPS with DNS pinning, no credentials, redirects, cookies or private-network destinations.

Successful identical lookups are reused for 24 hours. Changed controls do not relabel an old cache: its actual coordinates, category, radius and provider remain visible, with a previous-lookup notice. Failed or cancelled searches retain the previous cache and all saved leads. New searches are limited to once per minute; matching local cache reads remain available during that pause.

**Save researching lead** creates a local CRM record with provenance. Saving the same OSM object again reuses its active lead ID. The default view hides already tracked listings; clearing the checkbox shows them with a disabled Already tracked button. Saving does not contact the business, visit its website or send outreach. Community listings and missing website tags are research evidence, not verification that a business is operating or lacks a website.

Native live tests use isolated databases and public city coordinates in Austin and Bengaluru. The HTML status report links exact-source test, native UI and installer evidence. Regional replicas can lag or fail temporarily; errors are shown without substituting example listings.
